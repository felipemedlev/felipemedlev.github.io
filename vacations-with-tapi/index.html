Day by day

Fri 11 SepAthens
Land at 13:25. Metro to the centre takes 40 to 60 minutes, bus and taxi also work. Stay in Plaka, Koukaki, or near the Acropolis. Check in, then walk Plaka and Anafiotika, catch the sunset, and eat at a taverna.
Sat 12 SepAthens
Acropolis and Parthenon early, timed tickets are worth booking. Then the Acropolis Museum. Afternoon in the Ancient Agora or a walk through Psyrri and Thissio. Dinner nearby.
Sun 13 SepAthens to Sifnos
Morning ferry Piraeus → Sifnos. Arrive at Kamares port, transfer to Apollonia, Artemonas, Kastro, or a beach base. Settle in and explore close by.
Mon 14 to Wed 16Sifnos
Apollonia and Artemonas villages, Kastro for the medieval feel and the views, walking paths between villages or down to Chrysopigi Monastery, beaches at Platis Gialos and Vathi or quieter coves, pottery workshops, and very good local food such as chickpea dishes and seafood. A scooter or small car gives you freedom. Quiet and authentic.
Thu 17 SepSifnos to Milos
Short ferry Sifnos → Milos, 35 to 60 minutes, roughly 6 to 15 euro per person, several crossings a day. Arrive at Adamas. You can also move a day earlier on 16 Sep if you want more time on Milos.
Thu 17 to Sat 19Milos
Full day boat tour for the Kleftiko caves and the coastline, well worth it. Sarakiniko for the white volcanic rock, Firiplaka and other beaches, Pollonia fishing village, Plaka village at sunset, and Klima with the colourful boat houses. A scooter or car makes the beaches much easier.
Sat 19 SepMilos to Athens
Afternoon or evening ferry Milos → Piraeus. High speed is around 3 to 4 hours, conventional is longer, fares roughly 38 to 80 euro per person. From Piraeus head to the airport, or stay in the city if you want a break. Leave a buffer for delays.
Sun 20 SepHome
Flight departs Athens 00:30 and lands in Tel Aviv 02:30. Yom Kippur starts that evening around 18:18, so you have about 16 hours at home first.
Worth planning ahead
  • Book the Milos to Piraeus ferry on 19 Sep as early as possible. It is the one leg that has to work, since the flight is the same night.
  • Sifnos and Milos ferries can be cancelled in strong wind. Keep the last night flexible in case you need to return to Athens a day early.
  • Acropolis tickets are timed. Buy them before the trip and go for the first slot.
  • Book scooters or cars in advance for both islands.
