Day by day

Fri 11 SepAthens
Land at 13:25 on 6H567. Metro to the centre takes 40 to 60 minutes, bus and taxi also work. Stay in Plaka, Koukaki, or near the Acropolis. Check in, then walk Plaka and Anafiotika, catch the sunset, and eat at a taverna.
Sat 12 SepAthens
Acropolis and Parthenon early. Ticket links and details on section below. Can you check if you have time pls? Afternoon in the Ancient Agora or a walk through Psyrri and Thissio. Dinner nearby.
Sun 13 SepAthens to Sifnos
Morning ferry Piraeus → Sifnos. Arrive at Kamares port, then stay in Apollonia or Artemonas — the villages, not a beach resort. Settle in and walk from there.
Mon 14 to Tue 15Sifnos
Apollonia and Artemonas villages, pottery workshops, and very good local food such as chickpea dishes and seafood. On Tuesday, Kastro for the medieval feel and the views, walking down to Chrysopigi Monastery, then a beach afternoon at Platis Gialos or Vathi. A scooter or small car gives you freedom. Quiet and authentic.
Wed 16 SepSifnos to Milos
Morning ferry Sifnos → Milos. Drop the Sifnos scooter at Kamares, pick up at Adamas. Afternoon at Sarakiniko, then Plaka for sunset. Easy dinner in Adamas.
Thu 17 to Fri 18Milos
Thursday: full-day boat tour to Kleftiko caves and the coastline — leave the scooter in Adamas. If wind cancels it, swap Thursday and Friday. Friday: Firiplaka, Klima, and Pollonia, the spread-out land day. A scooter makes the beaches much easier, so would you hop with me on one?
Sat 19 SepMilos to ATH
Need to be at ATH by 21:30 — 3 hours before 6H570 at 00:30. Book an early or early-afternoon high-speed Milos → Piraeus (~€80 pp), not the last sailing of the day. Morning is easiest; a ~13:00 SeaJet landing ~16:30 still leaves X96 / Line 3 buffer. A 16:00–17:00 departure (land 19:30–20:30) does not. Drop the scooter at Adamas first, then X96 (~60–90 min) or Metro Line 3 to the airport. Backup: ~30 min Olympic/Sky Express hop Milos → ATH.
Sun 20 SepHome
6H570 departs Athens 00:30 and lands in Tel Aviv 02:30. Be at the airport by 21:30 the evening before. Yom Kippur starts that evening around 18:18, so you have about 16 hours at home first.

Getting around

AirportAthens
Metro, bus, or taxi to the city or to Piraeus. Metro to the centre is about an hour.
FerriesBetween islands
The best way to move around. Prefer daylight sailings since high speed boats are available. Book early, then check again a few days before: weather can change the timetable. Ferryhopper is the place to compare.
Piraeus → Sifnos~€70 pp
2.5 to 5 hours, high-speed often 2.5 to 3. Frequent, with morning (7:00–9:00) and afternoon sailings.
Sifnos ↔ Milos~€15 pp
A short hop: 50 minutes high-speed, up to 2 hours conventional. Several a day, easy either way.
Milos → Piraeus~€80 pp
Early or early-afternoon high-speed on 19 Sep — 3 to 4 hours, not the last sailing. A morning or ~13:00 boat lands mid-afternoon and still leaves time for X96 / Line 3. A 16:00–17:00 SeaJet (land 19:30–20:30) is too tight for ATH by 21:30. Backup: fly Milos → ATH (~30 min, Olympic/Sky Express).
Piraeus → ATHX96 / Line 3
From the port, X96 express bus (~60–90 min, runs all night) or Metro Line 3 straight to the airport. Be at ATH by 21:30 — 3 hours before 6H570.

Worth planning ahead
  • Flights are booked: Kiwi 833295991, Israir 6H567 out and 6H570 back. On 19 Sep take an early or early-afternoon Milos → Piraeus high-speed — not the last ferry — then X96 or Metro Line 3. Be at ATH by 21:30 (3 hours before 00:30).
  • Sifnos and Milos ferries can be cancelled in strong wind. Keep a Milos → ATH flight (~30 min) as backup, and recheck times a few days before you travel.
  • Acropolis tickets are timed. Buy them before the trip and go for the first slot: the site is €30 (Digital Guide app included) and the museum is €20.
  • Book scooters or cars in advance for both islands.
  • Cards work almost everywhere. Carry a little cash for small shops and taxis.
