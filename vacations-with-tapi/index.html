Buffer for delays

Sifnos is the quiet one: villages, hiking, and food. Milos is the adventurous one: beaches, boat tours, volcanic rock. Both skip the crowds and prices of Santorini or Mykonos. The calendar uses 4 nights Sifnos + 2 Milos; the other good split is 3 + 3 (ferry on 16 Sep). Confirm sailings on Ferryhopper.

Day by day

Fri 11 SepAthens
Land at 13:25. Metro to the centre takes 40 to 60 minutes, bus and taxi also work. Stay in Plaka, Koukaki, or near the Acropolis. Check in, then walk Plaka and Anafiotika, catch the sunset, and eat at a taverna.
Sat 12 SepAthens
Acropolis and Parthenon early, its recommended to book tickets in advanced, can you check if you have time pls?. Then the Acropolis Museum. Afternoon in the Ancient Agora or a walk through Psyrri and Thissio. Dinner nearby.
Sun 13 SepAthens to Sifnos
Morning ferry Piraeus → Sifnos. Arrive at Kamares port, transfer to Apollonia, Artemonas, Kastro, or a beach base. Settle in and explore close by.
Mon 14 to Wed 16Sifnos
Apollonia and Artemonas villages, Kastro for the medieval feel and the views, walking paths between villages or down to Chrysopigi Monastery, beaches at Platis Gialos and Vathi or quieter coves, pottery workshops, and very good local food such as chickpea dishes and seafood. A scooter or small car gives you freedom. Quiet and authentic.
Thu 17 SepSifnos to Milos
Short ferry Sifnos → Milos. Arrive at Adamas. You can also move a day earlier on 16 Sep if you want more time on Milos.
Thu 17 to Sat 19Milos
Full day boat tour for the Kleftiko caves and the coastline. Sarakiniko for the white volcanic rock, Firiplaka and other beaches, Pollonia fishing village, Plaka village at sunset, and Klima with the colourful boat houses. A scooter makes the beaches much easier, so would you hop with me on one?
Sat 19 SepMilos to Athens
Afternoon or evening ferry Milos → Piraeus. High speed is around 3 to 4 hours, conventional is longer, fares around 80€ per person. From Piraeus head to the airport, or stay in the city if you want a break.
Sun 20 SepHome
Flight departs Athens 00:30 and lands in Tel Aviv 02:30. Yom Kippur starts that evening around 18:18, so you have about 16 hours at home first.

Getting around

AirportAthens
Metro, bus, or taxi to the city or to Piraeus. Metro to the centre is about an hour.
FerriesBetween islands
The best way to move around. Prefer daylight sailings since high speed boats are available. Book early, then check again a few days before: weather can change the timetable. Ferryhopper is the place to compare.
Piraeus → Sifnos~€70 pp
2.5 to 5 hours, high-speed often 2.5 to 3. Frequent, with morning (7:00–9:00) and afternoon sailings.
Sifnos ↔ Milos~€15 pp
A short hop: 50 minutes high-speed, up to 2 hours conventional. Several a day, easy either way.
Milos → Piraeus~€80 pp
High-speed often 3 to 4 hours. Daily morning and afternoon options. This is the tight one before the night flight.
On the islands
A car or scooter is the easiest way to explore both Sifnos and Milos.
Worth planning ahead
  • Book flights, hotels, and ferries early. The Milos to Piraeus ferry on 19 Sep is the one leg that has to work, since the flight is the same night.
  • Sifnos and Milos ferries can be cancelled in strong wind. Keep the last night flexible in case you need to return to Athens a day early, and recheck times a few days before you travel.
  • Acropolis tickets are timed. Buy them before the trip and go for the first slot.
  • Book scooters or cars in advance for both islands.
  • Cards work almost everywhere. Carry a little cash for small shops and taxis.
